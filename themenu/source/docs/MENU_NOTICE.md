# Price and menu information notice

The source copy is in `content/menu-notice.json`; `scripts/build_menu_notice.py` generates `assets/menu-notice.js` for the interactive app. Static HTML, Markdown, and discovery outputs read the same source. A short notice appears on the directory. Every restaurant detail view shows the main warning above any dishes or prices, including verified menus, excerpts, stale menus, and unavailable menus. The main warning is visible by default. Supplementary details use a native keyboard-accessible disclosure.

## Visible notice

**Prices & menu availability**

Prices, menu items, and availability can change without notice. We do our best to keep information current, but updates may be delayed and errors or omissions can occur. We cannot guarantee that listings are current or complete. Please confirm current prices and menu details directly with the restaurant before ordering.

## Supplementary details

Menus are provided for reference. They are not a live price feed or a price quote from theMenu. Prices may vary by location, time, or ordering method and may exclude taxes, fees, and optional extras.

A last-verified date records when information was checked. It does not guarantee that the restaurant has made no changes since. Some listings are excerpts and may omit items, options, or specials.

## Scope

This is a factual product disclosure, not a promise of legal immunity or a complete set of terms of use. No blanket waiver of responsibility, liability cap, or mandatory arbitration clause is introduced. Counsel should assess any binding liability limitations before launch. Verification, source permission, correction review, and honest freshness labels still apply.

The placement follows the FTC's guidance that material disclosures should be clear and near the information they qualify; the same guidance explains that disclaimers cannot cure misleading claims.
