    Sources, snapshot dates, and how to read this view

    This local prototype reads bundled files. Opening the page does not request weather for your private spot. The map shows named city samples; nearby weather is regional context, not a forecast measured at your property. Saved spots stay in this browser.

    U.S. values are hourly NWS forecast periods. ECCC values are hourly city forecasts, treated as one-hour samples. Canadian hourly coverage is about 24 hours in this feed; later dates remain blank. A partial day is labeled with the hours available. Air below freezing does not establish ice on a wetland.

    Reports and ground observations keep their actual dates regardless of the selected forecast day. NOAA's snow analysis combines modeling with observations; transparent pixels include shallow snow and missing coverage. NCEI daily station records can lag by several days. USGS stage is relative to each gauge's local reference and cannot establish marsh depth. Provisional values can change.

    Different survey methods, species groups, and places should not be added together as a flyway population. Iowa's rounded mallard percentage cannot be converted into an exact mallard count.

    Data: National Weather Service. Data Source: Environment and Climate Change Canada · licence. Snow analysis: NOAA NOHRSC. Daily history: NOAA NCEI. River observations: USGS · provisional-data statement. Contains adapted data; no agency endorsement is implied. Geography: Natural Earth, public domain. U.S. town search: GeoNames, CC BY 4.0.

    No eBird or BirdCast records are included. NOAA surface-front positions are shown only at their stated valid times, without interpolation or a local arrival prediction. Pressure time series, winds aloft, and a duck migration model are still to connect. Weather, ground observations, and official report sources are checked daily. Validated numeric survey formats import automatically; newer reports needing review and unavailable checks are shown separately. A source check never changes a survey observation date.
