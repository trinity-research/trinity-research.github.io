      HNTED by Trinity Research.

      HNTED is a field-planning project from Trinity Research LLC. This web edition carries forward the sun-and-moon hunting rating formulas recovered from Time2Hunt Lite 4.1 by Bad Daddy Apps. A daily rating weighs lunar transit near sunrise or sunset, the moon’s maximum altitude, and its distance from Earth. Hourly ratings blend lunar events with sunrise, sunset, and midday.

      Sun and moon positions are calculated on your device with SunCalc. Astronomy inputs have been refreshed, so results may differ from the old Android app. All times use the selected spot’s time zone, including daylight saving time.

      Ratings are a planning aid, not a prediction of animal behavior. Weather forecasts and pressure comparisons are planned for a future update. Wildlife observations are not yet included. No account is needed; spots and preferences are saved only in this browser. Town search uses a bundled U.S. town list. Searches and selected coordinates are processed on your device for the sun, moon, and rut outlook.

      At polar latitudes, a sun or moon event may not occur on a given date. A dash means there is no event that day. Hunting ratings are unavailable during continuous daylight or darkness.
