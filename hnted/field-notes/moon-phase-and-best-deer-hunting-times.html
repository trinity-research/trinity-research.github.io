Can the moon tell you the best time to hunt deer?

Trinity Research LLC · Published November 1, 2026 · 2 min read

I've used hunting-time guides before, including an old app and a paper wheel. I like having something to plan around. I also want to understand what those predictions are based on.

What is the moon guide measuring?

Moon phase and moon position are different things. Phase tells us how much of the visible moon is lit. Position tells us when it rises, passes overhead, sets, or moves underfoot. Before comparing two guides, I would check which of those events each one is highlighting.

Mississippi State followed 48 GPS-collared bucks over two years in central Mississippi. Its Extension report tested several popular moon ideas and did not find a meaningful activity improvement associated with those predictions. That is a reason to be careful about what we promise, while remembering where and how the study was done. [1]

Another study in South Carolina did find some lunar associations, but they changed with phase and did not line up neatly with solunar-chart claims. To me, that means there is more to investigate. It does not give us a dependable promise about when a buck will walk past. [2]

What does our number mean?

HNTED carries forward the formulas recovered from the old Time2Hunt app. The number describes astronomical timing and weighting rules. An 80 does not mean an 80 percent chance of seeing a deer. I want that distinction to be easy to understand when someone first opens the forecast.

I would keep the moon windows alongside sunrise, sunset, local rut timing, and the weather. Then someone can look at the hours they actually have available. A lower moon score should not make a good local rut window disappear from the top of the screen.

Give it a fair test

The useful question is whether these windows help on our ground. Cameras could let us compare independent visits inside and outside the highlighted periods, while keeping time of day, season, weather, and camera uptime in view. Include the quiet days and several lunar cycles. That would give us something we can check instead of simply assuming the guide is right.
